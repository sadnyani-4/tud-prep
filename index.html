Sports & Health Knowledge Base

A. Sports Medicine & Physiology

  • Musculoskeletal: Active (muscles) vs. Passive (bones/ligaments) systems.
  • Energy Metabolism: Aerobic vs. Anaerobic pathways (ATP-CP, Glycolysis).
  • Neurophysiology: Motor unit recruitment and signal transduction.

B. Training & Movement Science

  • Biomechanics: Kinematics (motion) vs. Kinetics (forces).
  • Performance Diagnostics: VO2 Max testing and Lactate thresholds.
  • Training Control: Internal vs. External workload monitoring (RPE vs. GPS data).
