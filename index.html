Part 1: Sports & Health High-Yield Notes

1. Energy Metabolism (Crucial)

Anaerobic Alactic: High intensity (0-10s). Uses ATP & Creatine Phosphate.

Anaerobic Lactic: High intensity (10-90s). Produces Lactate; glucose breakdown without O2.

Aerobic: Low/Moderate intensity (>2 mins). Uses O2 to burn carbs and fats in mitochondria.

Exam Tip: Understand the "Lactate Threshold" – the point where lactate produced exceeds the clearance rate.

2. Biomechanics & Physics

Kinetics: Study of forces (Gravity, Ground Reaction Force, Friction).

Kinematics: Description of motion (Velocity, Acceleration, Angles) without forces.

Newton's 2nd Law in Sports: $F = m \times a$. Crucial for calculating force plates data in jumps.
